## Our Standard

CareCanvas contributors collaborate across healthcare, design, engineering, and
accessibility disciplines. We expect respectful, specific, and constructive
participation.

Positive behavior includes:

* Welcoming people with different experience levels and backgrounds
* Giving actionable technical feedback without personal attacks
* Respecting privacy, consent, and healthcare data boundaries
* Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
* Keeping discussions focused on user outcomes and repository scope
* Considering accessibility and inclusive language

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted attention
* Publishing another person's private information
* Sharing patient data, credentials, or confidential healthcare information
* Deliberately disrupting issues, reviews, or community spaces
* Using sexualized language or imagery
* Retaliating against someone who raises a concern in good faith

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and other spaces where someone represents the CareCanvas project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not
include sensitive personal or patient information in a public issue.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [Security](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
policy. Responses should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety
of affected participants.

Maintainers should protect the privacy of reporters and share details only with
people needed to assess and resolve the concern.
